# Design Direction

## Project

Weather Outfit Planner

## Final Direction

The final design direction is a **playful editorial weather brief**.

The page should feel like a mix of:

- a personal weather passport
- a fashion recommendation card
- a colorful editorial magazine page

The goal is to make the tool feel useful and polished, but also a little fun and memorable.

The interface should not look like a normal weather dashboard.

The weather supports the decision.

**The outfit recommendation is the main answer.**

---

# Design Goal

The user should feel like they are receiving a small personalized style brief for their destination.

The visual hierarchy should be:

1. What is the tool?
2. Where and when am I going?
3. What should I wear?
4. What are the local weather conditions?
5. Why was this outfit recommended?
6. What should I bring?

The outfit should always feel more important than the raw weather numbers.

---

# Design Personality

The final interface should feel:

- playful
- cute
- colorful
- editorial
- friendly
- slightly quirky
- organized
- modern

It should still look professional enough for a class project.

It should not feel:

- childish
- cluttered
- like a cartoon
- like a standard weather app
- overly animated
- overly complicated

- dark border

## Hero Heading

Main copy:

**Forecast first.  
Dress second.**

The heading should be:

- very large
- serif
- dark ink
- tightly spaced
- editorial

## Decorative Element

# Form Controls

Inputs should use:

- white background
- dark outline
- rounded corners
- clean sans-serif text

When focused, the input should slightly rise and receive a blue offset shadow.

This creates a playful interaction without adding complicated animation.

- dark text
- dark outline

This gives the button a physical sticker/button feeling.

# Outfit Row Styling

Rows should look like small colorful cards.

Use alternating:

- pale blue
- pale pink

Each row should have:

- rounded corners
- small spacing between rows
- subtle border

The label appears smaller.

The recommendation appears stronger and darker.

This makes the exact outfit easy to scan.

---

# Local Weather Explanation

Below the outfit, include a short explanation of why the recommendation was made.

Example:

> At your selected time in Boston, Massachusetts, it should feel like 48°F with 70% humidity, a 40% precipitation chance, and winds around 12 mph.

Additional explanation may mention:

- rain
- snow
- humidity
- strong wind
- breathable materials
- waterproof footwear

This explanation appears inside a pale yellow card.

---

# Local Conditions Card

The weather card appears beside the outfit recommendation on desktop.

Heading:

`LOCAL CONDITIONS`

The card should use:

- pale blue background
- rounded corners
- dark border
- offset shadow
- very slight rotation

The slight rotation gives the card a playful printed-card appearance.
